Methodological Framework of the Strategic Alignment

The EUNetCCC pilot project (Romagna Pilot IRST-AUSL) is structured around 6 active work dimensions covering the fundamental aspects of integrating and strengthening the local oncological network. In order to align the management effort with the standards of the European consortium CraNE D7.2 (Set of criteria and standards for EU CCCs), each of the 29 milestones scheduled in the timeline has been mapped. This bidirectional association demonstrates how the operational integration activities in Romagna serve as the practical vehicle to achieve compliance with European requirements and, conversely, how the European requirements provide a robust quality guide for the project's milestones.

Analysis Note on Prevention (Dimension 6): In the pilot's operational timeline, primary and secondary prevention activities are not managed by explicit milestones but are instead integrated into already existing local and regional institutional healthcare processes. Consequently, CraNE Topic 5 (Prevention) reflects this organizational characteristic, showing a coverage to be mapped through standard corporate channels.
